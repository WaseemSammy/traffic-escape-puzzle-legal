Privacy Policy

Traffic Escape Puzzle  ·  Effective 1 September 2026

The short version. Traffic Escape Puzzle has no accounts and no servers. We do not collect, transmit or store any personal information about you. Your progress stays on your device. The app does show ads and offer in-app purchases, and those services — provided by Google and Apple — collect device information of their own, which is described below.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the mobile game Traffic Escape Puzzle ("the app", "the game") on Android and iOS. In this policy "we" and "us" mean the developer of the app. You can reach us at sharpapptech@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

None. The app does not ask for, collect or transmit any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • account credentials — the game has no sign-in or user accounts;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files;
  • analytics or crash telemetry.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so it can be there when you come back. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us:

  • which levels you have unlocked and completed;
  • stars earned and your best move count per level;
  • your coin balance;
  • sound, music and vibration settings;
  • daily reward streak and daily challenge completion;
  • whether you have purchased Remove Ads.

It is held in the operating system's standard app preference storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Because it is only on your device, we cannot access, export or delete it for you.

4. Third-party services

Two categories of third-party service operate inside the app. They collect data under their own privacy policies, not ours.

Google AdMob — advertising

The app shows banner ads during play, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch in exchange for coins, hints or extra undos. To do this, Google AdMob may collect and process:

Google Play Billing and Apple StoreKit — purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iOS. We never see or store your payment card details. The app only learns whether a purchase succeeded, so it can unlock what you bought. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

Limiting ad personalisation

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time, which prevents ads from being personalised using past activity:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track; or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalised Ads.

Removing ads entirely

The Remove Ads purchase stops banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch one for a bonus.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. All locally stored progress and settings are removed with it.

6. Children

Traffic Escape Puzzle is intended for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at sharpapptech@gmail.com and we will act on it.

7. Data retention

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. On-device data persists until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data held by Google or Apple is retained according to their own policies.

8. Security

Because the app stores nothing on our side and transmits nothing to us, there is no account of yours for us to lose. On-device data is protected by your operating system's standard app sandboxing.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data held about you — including access, correction, deletion and objecting to processing. As we hold no personal data, such requests are best directed to Google or Apple, whose services process the data described in section 4. You are welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
